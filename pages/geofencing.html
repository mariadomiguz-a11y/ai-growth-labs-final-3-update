Geofencing Marketing

Reach customers the moment they're near your business — or your competitor's. Geofencing delivers targeted mobile ads to people in specific locations.

Get Your Geofencing Strategy →

Target Customers Where They Are

Geofencing lets you draw virtual boundaries around any location — your business, a competitor, an event, or an entire neighborhood — and serve ads to people within that area.

When potential customers walk near your competitor, they see YOUR ad on their phone. When they visit a local event, they learn about YOUR business. This hyper-local targeting delivers conversion rates 2-3x higher than traditional digital ads.
