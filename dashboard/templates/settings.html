📋 Integration Guide

🤖 AI Providers

Powers: SEO audits, content generation, competitor analysis, AI chat assistant. Need at least ONE AI key.

📧 SMTP

Powers: Email reports to clients, payment reminders, notification emails. Gmail App Password works.

📞 Twilio

Powers: Inbound/outbound calls, AI voice agent, SMS alerts, call recording. $1/mo + per-call cost.

💬 WhatsApp

Powers: Client updates via WhatsApp, report sharing, appointment reminders. Needs Meta Business account.

🔔 Slack

Powers: Team notifications on new leads, task completions, payments received. Free with any Slack plan.

💳 Stripe

Powers: Recurring billing, auto-invoicing, payment processing, subscription management. 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction.

📊 Google Search Console

Powers: Real keyword rankings, click data, impression data, CTR tracking. Free — needs OAuth setup.

🔄 Demo Mode

All integrations work in demo mode without API keys — realistic sample data is returned. Add real keys for production.
